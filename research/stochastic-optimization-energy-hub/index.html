Stochastic Optimization in Multi-Carrier Energy Hub

Robust operational planning for integrated energy systems with electrical energy storage degradation.

Overview

This study develops a stochastic optimization model for the short-term operation of a Multi-Carrier Energy Hub. The model coordinates electricity, heating, cooling, and natural gas while considering uncertain demand and energy prices.

A key contribution is the explicit inclusion of electrical energy storage degradation in the scheduling process, allowing the economic cost of battery use to be considered alongside energy operating costs.

Multi-Carrier Energy Hub Stochastic Optimization Energy Storage Long-Term Planning

System Model

Energy Hub

The modeled hub includes combined heat and power, an electrical heat pump, a boiler, and an absorption chiller.

Energy Storage

An electrical energy storage system is scheduled for charging and discharging, with degradation linked to battery usage and operating conditions.

Optimization Goal

Minimize the hub's operating cost while satisfying electrical, heating, and cooling demand across the planning horizon.

Results

Selected demand and energy-flow results from the multi-carrier energy hub optimization study.

Key Result

7.4% improvement in economic performance

Demand uncertainty is represented through generated operating scenarios and scenario reduction. Compared with the reference planning approach, the proposed framework achieved a 7.4% improvement in economic performance while accounting for battery degradation.
