MASTER'S THESIS

Probabilistic Energy Routing for Reliability Evaluation of Energy Internet

🎓 SHARIF UNIVERSITY OF TECHNOLOGY

M.Sc. Thesis in Energy Systems Engineering

This research introduces a probabilistic energy routing framework for Energy Internet architectures considering uncertainties in renewable generation, electrical demand, and transmission line availability.

Energy Internet Reliability Graph Theory Monte Carlo Optimization Python

Overview

Energy Internet is expected to support large-scale integration of renewable energy resources. However, uncertainty in distributed generation and component failures makes deterministic routing insufficient.

This thesis proposes a Probabilistic Energy Routing (PER) algorithm capable of selecting reliable energy paths while incorporating stochastic behavior of loads, renewable generation, and network failures.

Research Contributions

  • Introduced the Probabilistic Energy Routing (PER) algorithm.
  • Developed stochastic models for electrical loads and line failures.
  • Integrated Monte Carlo Simulation with routing analysis.
  • Evaluated reliability using EENS and RRW indices.
  • Validated the methodology on IEEE distribution systems.

Methodology

The framework combines graph-based routing, probabilistic component modeling, and Monte Carlo simulation to estimate the reliability of energy delivery under uncertainty.

Results

The proposed approach demonstrated improved routing decisions under uncertain operating conditions and provided a quantitative assessment of network reliability compared with deterministic routing strategies.
