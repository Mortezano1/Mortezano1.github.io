About me

I am Morteza Kolivandi, a Digital Energy Engineer passionate about making energy systems smarter, more reliable, and data-driven through artificial intelligence.

My academic research focuses on intelligent energy systems, particularly the Energy Internet, where I investigated energy routing algorithms ranging from classical graph-based approaches to nature-inspired and decentralized methods, together with reliability assessment and machine-learning-based fault detection and diagnosis.

These interdisciplinary studies have resulted in research contributions spanning Energy Internet reliability analysis, renewable energy technologies, and AI-driven solutions for modern energy systems.

Currently, I develop the energy intelligence layer of a photovoltaic power plant monitoring platform, focusing on performance analysis, energy forecasting, anomaly detection, fault diagnosis, KPI evaluation, and operational optimization. The solution is being deployed in a 7.5 MW photovoltaic power plant in Mahallat, Iran, bridging academic research with real-world applications in sustainable energy systems.
